Welcome to Simlaya Inn

A Tranquil Boutique Haven in the Heart of Hikkaduwa

Located along the peaceful lane of Wallawwaththa in Hikkaduwa, Simlaya Inn is a boutique villa and homestay designed for travelers who appreciate clean, tasteful spaces, natural tranquility, and heartfelt personal service.

Unlike crowded beachfront mega-hotels, our boutique property offers a quiet sanctuary tucked away from traffic noise — yet just an effortless 750-meter stroll (< 1 km) to Hikkaduwa Beach and a 3-minute walk to the railway station.
